What's inside

A clipboard that remembers. A voice that types.

Clipboard+ Desktop lives quietly in your menu bar, system tray or top bar. Each tool has its own shortcut, and you choose which ones to turn on.

01 Clipboard history

Everything you copied, one search away.

Press Super+Shift+F and start typing. Enter copies the top match; click anything to copy it back.

  • Text, links and images, newest first
  • Star favorites; filter by images or text
  • Skips what password managers mark as secret
  • Pause capture for an hour, or until you resume
02 Voice dictation

Talk instead of type, in any app.

Press Super+Shift+D, speak, press it again. Your words are on the clipboard, and in your history too.

  • Free on-device Whisper model, a one-time 148 MB download
  • English, or multilingual with automatic detection
  • Or bring your own OpenAI, Groq or compatible API
  • Turn it off if you only want the clipboard

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Contact us.

Do I need a Clipboard+ account?

No. Clipboard+ Desktop works fully on its own: your history stays on your computer, offline, with no sign-up. Sign in, or paste an API key, only if you want the same history on your Mac, Windows and Linux computers and in the Clipboard+ browser extension.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account and no subscription. On-device transcription costs nothing. Only if you choose an external service such as OpenAI or Groq might that service charge you.

Does my voice or clipboard leave my computer?

Not by default. Transcription runs on your computer with a local Whisper model, and your clipboard history is stored on your computer. Text and links are sent only if you connect a Clipboard+ account; images and audio are never uploaded.

Which systems does it run on?

Linux (Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch and openSUSE, including GNOME on Wayland), macOS, and 64-bit Windows. It lives in the top bar, menu bar or system tray and starts at login. Linux is the most tested; macOS and Windows support is newer.

What are the shortcuts?

Dictation: Super+Shift+D on Linux, Win+Shift+D on Windows, ⌃⌥⇧D on a Mac. Clipboard history: the same with F. You can change both in Settings.

How does sync work?

In Settings, connect your Clipboard+ account with your email and password, or with an API key from your account. Text and links you copy then show up on every computer where Clipboard+ Desktop is signed in, in the browser extension, and on the website. Images stay on the device they came from. “Clear history” can clear everywhere at once.

I signed up for Clipboard+ with Google. How do I connect?

On your Clipboard+ Account page, click Create a key for Clipboard+ Desktop, then choose Use an API key instead in the app and paste it.

Which languages can I dictate in?

English, or multilingual with automatic language detection. Whisper recognizes dozens of languages.

Can I use my own transcription API?

Yes. Choose “My own AI service” and use OpenAI, Groq, or any OpenAI-compatible /audio/transcriptions endpoint. Your key is stored only on your computer.

How do I stop or remove it?

Choose Quit from the app’s icon, and turn off Open at Login in the same menu so it doesn’t start again. For a full removal, contact us and we’ll walk you through it.
